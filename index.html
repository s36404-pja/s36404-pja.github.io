This unofficial Wiki is dedicated to the game Subnautica 2

Welcome to the unofficial Subnautica 2 fauna Wiki!

This website compiles information about all the different animals that can be found in the game.
You can access the data log entries from the game's own PDA, through the "Browse" button
or the summarised contents list below.

  • Cage Gorgon
  • Cerathecan
  • Coral Crab
  • Listening Gorgon
